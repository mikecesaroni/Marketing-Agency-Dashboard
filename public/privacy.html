Privacy Policy

The Working Class Marketing · The CRM · Last updated 29 August 2026

The Working Class Marketing — a trade name of The Roundtable LLC — runs marketing campaigns for home-services businesses. The Working Class Marketing CRM (“the CRM”) is the internal tool we use to do that work: it holds our client records, pulls advertising performance figures from the platforms we advertise on, builds ad creative, and tracks invoicing. This policy explains what data passes through it, why, and who else sees it.

The CRM is not a consumer product. It is used by The Working Class Marketing staff and by the business clients who hire us. It has no public sign-up, it does not sell data, and it does not advertise to the people whose information it holds.

1. What we collect

Client business information

For each business we work with: trading and legal business name, industry, service area, website, advertising budget, fees and contract dates. Where a client completes our onboarding or account-setup forms, we also collect the business phone number, support email address, and the name, email address and phone number of the authorised representative who signs off on the account. That representative's details are personal data, and we collect them because the advertising and messaging platforms we set up on the client's behalf require a named, contactable person.

Advertising data from Meta

Through the Meta Marketing API, using a System User access token that our clients authorise by assigning us their ad accounts, we read and store:

  • Ad account, Facebook Page and pixel identifiers.
  • Campaign, ad set and ad names, identifiers and delivery status.
  • Aggregate performance figures — amount spent, impressions, clicks, and the number of leads recorded, by day and by ad.
  • The definitions of instant lead forms attached to a client's Page.

We do not retrieve the contents of leads. When someone fills in an instant form on one of our clients' ads, their name, phone number and email address stay with Meta and flow to the client's own systems. The CRM reads how many leads a form received; it never reads what anyone typed into one. We hold no consumer contact details from advertising.

We also use the Meta Marketing API to create and change advertising objects — campaigns, ad sets, creatives and ads — in the ad accounts our clients have assigned to us. Everything the tool creates is created paused, and a person switches it on.

Creative files and Google Drive

We store the images and documents uploaded to a client's record, and the ad creative our studio produces. Where a client shares a Google Drive folder with us, we read that folder through a Google service account with read-only access so their photographs can be placed into ads directly. We do not modify, move or delete anything in a client's Drive, and we read only the folder they have shared.

Billing

Payment records — amount, date, method and status — are recorded against each client. Payments are processed by Stripe, not by us; we never see or store full card numbers. Where a payment arrives that we cannot match to a client, we retain the customer name and email address Stripe reports so we can attribute it correctly.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking cookies on this site.
  • No analytics profiling of visitors. Any pixel referenced in the tool belongs to the client and runs on the client's own website under the client's own policy.
  • No consumer contact details from lead forms, as set out above.
  • No payment card data.

2. Why we use it

Solely to deliver the service our clients pay us for: planning, building, publishing and reporting on advertising campaigns; producing creative; setting up the platforms a campaign needs; and invoicing. We do not sell data, share it for anyone else's marketing, or use it to build profiles of individuals.

3. Who else sees it

Data reaches these providers because the service cannot function without them:

  • Meta Platforms — to read performance figures and to create and manage advertising in our clients' ad accounts.
  • Google — to read a client's shared Drive folder. Local Services Ads figures, where a client runs them, are entered by hand from the client's own reporting; we hold no Google Ads API access.
  • Supabase — our database, file storage and server functions.
  • Netlify — hosting for the application itself.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Anthropic — the assistant and reporting features in the tool send client business details and campaign performance figures to Anthropic's API to draft copy, analyse results and answer questions about an account. Those requests are not used to train models.
  • Resend — where monthly report emails are enabled, to deliver them.

We disclose data otherwise only where the law requires it. We do not transfer client data to other advertisers or data brokers.

4. Meta Platform Data

Data obtained through Meta's APIs is used only to operate and report on advertising for the client who granted access. We do not transfer it to any data broker, use it to build or augment profiles for advertising elsewhere, or retain it after it stops serving that purpose. If a client removes our access in Meta Business Manager, we stop collecting immediately and delete the advertising data we hold for them on request.

5. How long we keep it

Client records and campaign history are kept for as long as we work with that client and for up to seven years afterwards, which is what our tax and accounting obligations require. Advertising performance figures and creative files are deleted on request at any time, or within 90 days of an engagement ending if they are no longer needed for billing or reporting.

6. Deleting your data

Email ethan@horizonhvacinc.com with the business name and what you would like removed. We will confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain billing records.

You may also ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw a Google Drive folder or a Meta ad account assignment at any time — revoking access on the platform itself takes effect immediately, without needing to contact us first.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS and stored with our providers named above. Access tokens and API credentials are held as encrypted secrets on the server, never in the browser and never in our source code. Access to the tool is limited to The Working Class Marketing staff and, for their own records only, the client concerned.

8. Children

The CRM is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how client data is handled, tell affected clients directly.
